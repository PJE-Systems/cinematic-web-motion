# Accessible Motion

## Reduced motion

Respect:

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`

When enabled:

- remove large camera movements
- disable unnecessary parallax
- avoid rapidly changing effects
- show content immediately
- replace scrubbing with a static or lightly animated state

## Semantic content

Important information must remain accessible without Canvas, WebGL or video.

## Keyboard

Ensure:

- navigation works with keyboard
- focus is visible
- buttons and links remain standard interactive elements
- cinematic sections do not trap focus

## Contrast

Do not sacrifice text readability for cinematic imagery.

Use overlays only when necessary.

## Screen readers

Do not make a screen reader depend on visual animation.

Provide meaningful headings and text.

## Motion sensitivity

Avoid:

- rapid flashing
- high-frequency strobing
- excessive camera shake
- continuous large-scale movement behind text

## Fallback

A user who disables animation should still receive the complete experience and clear CTA.
