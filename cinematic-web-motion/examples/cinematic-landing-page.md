# Example: Premium Service Landing Page

This example demonstrates how to apply the skill without making the implementation specific to one company.

## Business

A nationwide service company coordinates local providers for customers and business clients.

## Core message

One central contact.
A large service network.
Fast coordination.

## Cinematic story

### Scene 1 - Establish

A vehicle moves through a realistic landscape.

Purpose:
Create a feeling of normal movement and establish the subject.

### Scene 2 - Disruption

The vehicle slows and stops.

Purpose:
Introduce the problem.

### Scene 3 - Freeze

The vehicle and environment freeze.

Purpose:
Create a visual pause and attention point.

### Scene 4 - Solution

The coordination system is introduced.

Purpose:
Show that the company takes control of the problem.

### Scene 5 - Scale

The camera pulls away from the vehicle and reveals a national network.

Purpose:
Communicate reach and coordination.

### Scene 6 - Resolution

The camera returns to the vehicle. Assistance arrives and movement resumes.

Purpose:
Show the outcome.

### Scene 7 - Conversion

The cinematic sequence transitions into normal website content:

- services
- business customers
- network/partners
- trust
- contact

## Implementation

A suitable implementation might be:

- generated/filmed video + scroll scrubbing
- or a frame sequence rendered from 3D
- GSAP ScrollTrigger for the timeline
- semantic HTML overlay
- static poster fallback
- simplified mobile version

Three.js should only be used if the scene itself requires real-time 3D.

## Visual direction

Aim for:

- premium automotive-commercial quality
- realistic environments
- controlled camera
- cinematic light
- restrained color treatment
- smooth continuous movement

Avoid:

- towing clichés
- cheap 3D
- neon
- sci-fi interfaces
- random particles
- fake terminal windows
- generic SaaS visuals

## Content rule

The story should be adapted to the actual company.

Do not invent:

- partner counts
- customer names
- coverage claims
- response times
- certifications
- awards
- statistics

Only use verified information from the project or provided sources.
